Customer service guide

How service works. To request service, sign in to your account portal.

Step by step

  1. Tell us the service address or ZIP code, yard size and cleanup frequency.
  2. Eligible independent providers may choose to respond with their own quote and availability.
  3. Review the provider's service rate and any separately disclosed customer-paid platform fee.
  4. Accept only after reviewing the complete price, service scope and provider details.
  5. After service, confirm completion and use the support tools in your account if there is a concern.

Price transparency

Providers set their own service rates. The ByeByeDoody platform fee (20% of the total) is shown separately before you pay; it is not deducted from the provider's quoted rate. Payments are processed by Stripe.

Privacy

Service addresses and private notes are encrypted before they are stored. ByeByeDoody does not collect GPS data; if location tools are ever introduced, their purpose and permission settings will be explained before use.
