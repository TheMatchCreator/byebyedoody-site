Customer service guide

Bookings are not open yet. This guide is informational and does not submit a service request.

How the planned service will work

  1. Tell us the service address or ZIP code, yard size and cleanup frequency.
  2. Eligible independent providers may choose to respond with their own quote and availability.
  3. Review the provider's service rate and any separately disclosed customer-paid platform fee.
  4. Accept only after reviewing the complete price, service scope and provider details.
  5. After service, confirm completion and use the planned support process if there is a concern.

Price transparency

Providers are intended to set or negotiate their own service rates. The proposed platform fee will be shown separately before a customer confirms a booking; it will not be deducted from the provider's agreed rate.

Privacy

The marketplace is not currently collecting customer addresses or GPS data. If location tools are introduced, their purpose and permission settings will be explained before use.
