Owner launch checklist

Owner tools are not connected. This is a planning checklist, not an authenticated dashboard. Do not enter credentials or customer/provider data here.

Required gates before public launch

  1. Obtain California employment counsel review of the referral-agency model, actual operating practices, agreements and customer-paid platform fee disclosures.
  2. Obtain written City of Sacramento guidance on whether each planned residential, commercial or multifamily service scope and waste-handling method requires a franchise or authorized hauler.
  3. Confirm insurance requirements and obtain appropriate coverage before operations.
  4. Configure managed authentication, account recovery, role permissions, audit logging and secure data retention.
  5. Connect and test a production database with backups, access controls and privacy safeguards.
  6. Integrate payment processing in test mode first; validate refunds, disputes, duplicate webhook handling and provider payout flow before any live transaction.
  7. Implement opt-in GPS only for a defined purpose, with a manual address/ZIP alternative and retention limits.
  8. Test customer quotes, provider opt-in/opt-out, cancellations, completion evidence, support and incident handling.

Current safe operating boundary

The portal is a static preview. It does not authenticate users, store applications, dispatch work, collect GPS, process payments or authorize off-site waste transport. Keep those functions disabled until the required legal, municipal, security and vendor setup is complete.
