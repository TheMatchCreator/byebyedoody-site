Independent-provider approach

The planned model is a referral arrangement, subject to review by a California employment attorney. Providers are intended to operate independently, set or negotiate their own rates, use their own tools, serve other customers and freely accept or decline referrals.

Transparent platform pricing

The proposed platform fee is a separately disclosed customer-paid fee. It is not intended to be deducted from a provider's independently agreed service rate. The exact pricing and disclosures will be reviewed before checkout is enabled.

Location privacy

Any future location feature will require clear permission and will be limited to a stated service purpose. Customers and providers will have a non-GPS alternative where practical.

Waste-handling safeguards

Service areas and permitted waste handling must be confirmed with the City of Sacramento before the platform offers jobs that involve transporting or disposing of collected waste.
