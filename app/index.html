How payments work

When an independent provider accepts your job, you pay the provider's quoted rate plus a separately disclosed ByeByeDoody platform fee (15% of the total). Payments are processed by Stripe; ByeByeDoody never sees or stores your card details. Providers receive their full quoted rate through Stripe.

For general service inquiries, use the customer inquiry form. For provider information, visit Work with us.

Never email passwords, identity documents, Social Security numbers, bank details, or payment-card information.
