Independent provider guide

Apply from your account portal. ByeByeDoody reviews each application before approval. Never send identity documents, Social Security numbers, banking details or passwords by email; payout details are collected directly by Stripe.

Referral arrangement

ByeByeDoody operates a referral marketplace. A description on this page does not determine anyone's legal worker status.

  • You decide whether to participate and which referrals to consider.
  • You may accept or decline each opportunity without a platform penalty.
  • You set or negotiate your own service rates and terms.
  • You use your own tools and operate under your own business identity.
  • You remain free to serve your own customers and work with other referral sources.
  • You choose your availability and service area.

Before any provider can go live

Required eligibility, business registration, insurance, service scope, customer disclosures and City of Sacramento waste-handling requirements must be confirmed. The platform will not require hidden or continuous GPS tracking. Any job-related location sharing must be permission-based and clearly explained.
