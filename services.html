What to expect

  • You submit an inquiry with your location and service needs.
  • The independent provider decides whether to respond and sets or negotiates their own rate.
  • Service terms and lawful waste-handling arrangements must be agreed before any work begins.
  • A referral request is not a confirmed booking. Once a provider accepts, you can pay securely through our marketplace.

Service availability and final pricing depend on location, yard conditions, access, and scope. Waste collection and disposal are subject to applicable local requirements and confirmed arrangements.
