HOW IT WORKS

From sign-up to payout

01

Sign up in 2 minutes

Sign in with email or Google, open Provider tools and fill in the short independent-business application with your service ZIP codes. We review it quickly.

02

Turn on instant job alerts

Our AI matches every new request to providers serving that ZIP and emails you right away. Add as many ZIP codes as you like to see more jobs.

03

Quote fast with AI

Tap “Suggest a price with AI” for a fair local price, adjust it if you want, and send your quote. Early quotes are the ones customers see first.

04

Do the job

After the customer pays, you see the address and notes. Tap “On my way” (sharing your location is optional and only until you arrive), “Arrived”, then “Job done”.

05

Get paid

Set up payouts once with Stripe. You keep your full quoted rate; the customer pays ByeByeDoody's platform fee (20% of their total) on top.

06

Need help?

Tap “Ask ByeByeDoody AI” at the bottom of any page for step-by-step help, any time.

You run your own business

  • You set your own prices and your own hours.
  • You choose which jobs to quote and can skip any job with no penalty. Nobody assigns you work.
  • You use your own tools and your own business name, and you're free to keep your own customers and other work.
  • Location is never tracked in the background. It's shared only if you choose, only during a paid job, and deleted after 7 days.

What you need

  • Your own dog-waste cleanup business (a sole proprietorship is fine) and any local business registration or tax certificate that applies to you.
  • Your own tools and supplies, and any vehicle insurance you need.
  • Safe, respectful service: secure gates, pet safety, and lawful waste disposal.
  • A bank account for Stripe payouts (entered on Stripe, never sent to us).

ByeByeDoody is a referral marketplace. Joining does not make you an employee, and we can't guarantee a number of jobs or income; how much work you get depends on customer demand in your ZIP codes and your quotes. Never email Social Security numbers, ID images or bank details.
