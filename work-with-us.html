INDEPENDENT SERVICE PROVIDERS

Build your own cleanup business. Find referrals when you want them.

ByeByeDoody is preparing a referral network for independent dog-waste cleanup businesses serving the Sacramento area.

A REFERRAL NETWORK, NOT A JOB PROMISE

You stay in charge of your business.

The intended model is to connect customers with independent providers. Providers would operate under their own business name, bring their own tools, set or negotiate their own service rates and hours, maintain their own customers, and decide freely whether to accept each referral.

Actual eligibility and terms will be confirmed before any provider is activated. The model is subject to review by California employment counsel and applicable local requirements.

  1. 01
    Operate independently

    Maintain your own business, tools, clients, and service practices.

  2. 02
    Choose your opportunities

    Review the job details and decide whether to respond. No acceptance is required.

  3. 03
    Agree directly on service terms

    Provider rates and customer charges must be clear before a referral is confirmed.

What may be required before referrals begin

  • Your own independently established service business and business name.
  • Applicable local business registration or tax certificate.
  • Your own tools and supplies suitable for safe cleanup.
  • Any vehicle and insurance coverage required for the services you choose to offer.
  • Agreement to reasonable customer-property, animal-safety, sanitation, and privacy requirements.
  • Ability to decide your own availability, rates, service terms, and whether to accept a referral.
Important — not an active provider application

This page does not create an employment or contractor relationship, guarantee work, or authorize you to begin services on behalf of ByeByeDoody. Provider onboarding, verification, referral terms, and payment arrangements are not yet active. Do not email Social Security numbers, driver's-license images, bank details, or other sensitive documents.

How the platform fee is planned

The current design target is a separately disclosed customer-paid platform fee. The provider's independently agreed service rate is not intended to be reduced by that fee. The final fee formula and contracts remain subject to California legal and accounting review and may change before launch.
