How the network works

Customers request service and independent pros choose whether to take the job. Pros stay free to decide which jobs suit their business.

  1. 01
    Customer requests service

    Customer supplies the service address and cleanup details.

  2. 02
    Eligible providers choose whether to respond

    Approved pros in your ZIP code get alerts. Location is shared only during an active job, with permission.

  3. 03
    Terms and price are agreed

    You see the pro's rate plus the ByeByeDoody platform fee before you pay.

Payments and safety

Sign in securely with your email or Google. Providers are reviewed and approved before they can send quotes. Payments are processed by Stripe; ByeByeDoody never sees or stores your card details. Waste removal and disposal options will be limited to arrangements confirmed as lawful for the property and service type.
