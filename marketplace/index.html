BUILT WITH CARE

How the network is intended to work

The planned marketplace will help customers request service, receive provider offers, and coordinate a referral when both sides agree. Independent providers will remain free to decide whether a referral suits their business.

  1. 01
    Customer requests service

    Customer supplies the service address and cleanup details.

  2. 02
    Eligible providers choose whether to respond

    Matching is intended for providers who opted in and are available; location sharing will require permission.

  3. 03
    Terms and price are agreed

    Provider rates and any separately disclosed customer platform fee will be shown before a referral is confirmed.

Not open for marketplace transactions yet

Secure sign-in, provider verification, live location matching, payment processing, and job completion records are still under development. No payment is collected on this page. Waste removal and disposal options will be limited to arrangements confirmed as lawful for the property and service type.
