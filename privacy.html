Privacy notice

How we handle information you submit through this website.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This notice explains how ByeByeDoody handles information you submit through this website. ByeByeDoody is a referral platform that connects Sacramento-area households and properties with independent dog waste cleanup providers.

Information you provide

When you submit a quote request, partnership inquiry, provider interest form, or contact message, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, organization, and message. Please don't include sensitive personal information, such as Social Security numbers, financial account details, or government ID numbers.

Accounts and the marketplace

If you create an account, sign-in is handled by Auth0 (with your email or Google). Customers' service addresses and private notes are encrypted before we store them and are shown only to the provider whose job you have paid for. Approved providers in your area see only your ZIP code and non-sensitive job details (frequency, number of dogs, yard size) so they can decide whether to quote. Payments and provider payouts are processed by Stripe; we never see or store full card or bank details.

Location during jobs

Providers can choose to share their device's precise location with a customer only during a paid job, from tapping "On my way" until they tap "Arrived", "Job done" or "Stop sharing". It is never collected in the background or outside a job. The customer sees only the latest point. We use it only to show job progress and to help resolve service issues, and we delete location points after 7 days. Sharing is optional; a provider can still complete jobs without it.

AI features

The "Ask ByeByeDoody AI" helper and AI price suggestions are powered by Cloudflare Workers AI. Chat messages are sent to the AI to generate a reply and are not saved in your account or used by us for advertising; the conversation is cleared when you close the page. AI answers can be wrong and are not legal, tax or employment advice. Please don't type passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into the chat.

Reminders and referrals

If you choose "Email me when it's time for my next cleanup", we store your sign-in email (encrypted) to send those reminders; turn them off anytime in your account and we delete the stored address. If you share your referral link, we record which account the link came from so both of you can receive the referral credit. Credits have no cash value and only reduce ByeByeDoody's platform fee.

Job alert emails

Providers who turn on job alerts let us store their sign-in email address (encrypted) to email them new jobs in their service ZIP codes. Turning alerts off deletes the stored address. Emails are sent through Resend.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, check service availability in your area, discuss a possible service or provider relationship, and communicate with you about your request. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Service providers

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, and form submissions are delivered to us by email through Resend. These companies process information only as needed to run the site and deliver messages. If you request service, we may share the details needed to respond (such as your ZIP code, number of dogs, and yard size) with independent providers in your area.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and maintain reasonable business records, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No website or transmission method is completely secure.

Your choices

California residents have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information such as precise location (which we already use only for the job-progress purpose described above). We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can make a request in the account portal under Privacy & support, or by email. To ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email byebyedoody123@aol.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Advertising and cookies

Our public pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ad Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. We don't show ads inside your account pages.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself anytime under My account → Privacy & support → Delete my account. We remove your address, notes, ZIP codes, alert and reminder settings, and referral credits right away. Records of jobs that were paid are kept without your contact details, because payment and tax rules require them.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website and our services change. The effective date above shows when it was last updated.
